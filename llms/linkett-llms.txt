# Linkett

> Linkett is a digital out-of-home (DOOH) analytics platform. A proprietary WiFi sensor mounted at the screen passively gathers audience data from nearby smartphones — no app required — and a hosted dashboard reports impressions, dwell time, frequency and conversion rate, plus demographic and behavioural insight, so digital signage networks, agencies and advertisers can measure real-world campaign performance. Linkett is a WestonExpressions Inc. company with offices in Toronto, Ontario and Mountain View, California, and says it is used by 100+ digital out-of-home networks.
